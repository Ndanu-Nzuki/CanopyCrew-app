## Set 2 · 2026-11-06 · picked Field Guide, built
Brief: Canopy Crew: a mobile app for greening São Paulo with native Atlantic Forest plants. Home screen is "Sample sites": three real urban planting sites shown as large tappable entries, each with a photo of the place and its key conditions: "Compact Sidewalk Strip" (narrow sidewalk strip under overhead power lines, full sun, max 5 m tall), "Sunny Degraded Lot" (20–50 m² open vacant lot, full sun, up to 12 m), "Shaded Courtyard" (15–30 m² courtyard between mid-rise buildings, partial to full shade, up to 8 m). Each shows a small count of fitting species (e.g. "5 native species fit") and condition chips (sun icon, height limit, power-lines warning). Top right holds a "My Workspace" sign-in button. Mandatory palette in every direction: #183D2B deep forest green (logo, headings, primary buttons), #477A55 leaf green (links, secondary buttons, icons), #A8C5A0 sage (illustrations, decorative details), #E7EFDF pale mint (alternate section backgrounds), #F7F6EF warm off-white (main background), #D5A66B ochre gold (small highlights/accents only). Native iOS feel, Liquid Glass tab bar with four tabs: Sites, Catalogue, Workspace, About. Wordmark "Canopy Crew" at top.
1. Field Guide (picked): Botanical field-journal feel: New York serif headings in deep forest green, thin sage line-drawn leaf details, sites as tall photo cards with a pale-mint caption panel listing conditions like specimen labels.. design/sets/2026-11-06-2/1-field-guide.jpg
2. Canopy Photo: Photo-led and immersive: each site is a full-width edge-to-edge photograph with a dark forest-green gradient and white bold SF Pro title over it, ochre chips for sun and height, content scrolling under the glass tab bar.. design/sets/2026-11-06-2/2-canopy-photo.jpg
3. Site Plan: Urban-planner blueprint: each site drawn as a simple top-down plot diagram in sage and leaf green (strip, lot, courtyard footprints with dimensions), SF Mono numerals for area and height limits, crisp grid on off-white.. design/sets/2026-11-06-2/3-site-plan.jpg
4. Community Grove: Friendly and civic: SF Rounded type, soft rounded mint panels, a horizontal carousel of the three sites with round plant avatars of fitting species stacked below each, an ochre 'Start planting' highlight.. design/sets/2026-11-06-2/4-community-grove.jpg
